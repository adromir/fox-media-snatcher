FFmpeg Companion Setup

The companion app connects Firefox to your local FFmpeg installation to convert streams (HLS/DASH) and re-encode audio/video formats directly on your PC.

  1. Ensure FFmpeg is installed (detected on your system!).
  2. Open the native-host/ directory in the project folder.
  3. Double-click install_host.bat to register the native messaging host in Windows.
  4. Restart Firefox or click the Test Connection button below.
